Version Control

**Tasks to complete before this session: Create two new repos on Github:**

1. `yourusername`
  - Name it `yourusername`, replacing `yourusername` with your literal Github username;
  - Ensure *Add README* is **ON**.
  - Click *Create Repository*.

2. `yourusername.github.io`
  - Name it `yourusername.github.io`, again replacing `yourusername` with your literal Github username;
  - Ensure *Add README* is **ON**.
  - Click *Create Repository*.
